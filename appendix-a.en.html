Appendix A. Implementation, Training Settings, and Archive Verification

A.1. Repository Modules and Source Notes

The experiment code is public. Raw experimental trajectories, training logs, and model checkpoints are not distributed with this report.

The pinned AskAct-EvalRL repository (AskAct-EvalRL) separates deterministic evaluation infrastructure from reward and trainer modules. Task generation resides in askact/registry.py and askact/splits.py, nine-field trajectory scoring in askact/evaluator.py, deterministic bundle replay in askact/replay.py, and sequence-collision analysis in tools/distinguishability.py. Six modules implement reward and advantage estimators: askact/reward.py, askact/webshop_reward.py, askact/gigpo.py, askact/phi_baseline.py, askact/group_phi.py, and askact/outgroup.py. Four verl integration loops reside in training/verl/askact_agent_loop.py, training/verl/webshop_agent_loop.py, training/verl/gigpo.py, and training/verl/vpr.py. Where preserved source comments reflect early drafts, §6.2.1 states the exact discounted advantage sum implemented alongside askact/webshop_reward.py, §5.1 documents the independent initial-page runs in research/tools/webshop_intervene.py, and phi_baseline.py counts all preceding actions with nonempty blocked_reason.
