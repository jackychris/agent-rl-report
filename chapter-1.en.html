1. Introduction

Agent evaluations based solely on terminal completion miss how a policy reaches its final state. We train Qwen3-8B Instruct on Ask-or-Act email tasks first with SFT (SFT #1) and then with continued GRPO (SFT-GRPO #1). Continued GRPO solves all 20 held-out recipient-clarification and confirmation pairs and lowers premature write attempts from 19/20 to 0/20. When sharing thresholds move into a separate policy tool across 60 modified email tasks, the same checkpoint solves the exact same 50/60 tasks by querying that tool after blocked send attempts. Pre-write queries to the policy tool drop from 60/60 to 0/60.

We build controlled training comparisons around three routes on Qwen3-8B Instruct with thinking disabled and LoRA adapters (Hu et al., 2021), as summarized in Table 1.1.

Across Ask-or-Act, these three routes expose sharp separations between task completion and underlying execution behavior (§3.1–§4.2). Five follow-up interventions examine SFT demonstration diversity, epoch dose, premature penalties, relaxed PPO clipping, state baselines across three random seeds, and reweighted vs synthetic curricula. These comparisons isolate where each repair stalls or diverges (§4.3).

In WebShop, explicit subpage visit rewards drive pre-purchase visitation above 99%. Competing-product comparisons drop near zero, and target-purchase Booleans remain unchanged under conflicting subpage text (§5.1–§5.2). Group-relative normalization diagnostics then reveal equal-return prompt groups, duplicate sampled trajectories, discounting penalties on two-step detours, shrinkage overlap in state anchors, and rollout-to-actor divergence (§6.1–§6.2).

Contributions.

  • We separate task completion from pre-write policy queries, operation intent, and returned-identifier passing across 1,780 deterministic Ask-or-Act tasks (§3.1–§4.2).
  • We evaluate five training interventions spanning supervised diversity, epoch dose, penalties, clipping, state baselines, and synthetic curricula, and map 150 archived policies by action-sequence collision (§4.3).
  • We measure process-reward visitation, browsing depth, purchased-attribute location, and independent text treatments on WebShop, then isolate tied groups, GiGPO discounting, shrinkage overlap, and trainer-rollout mismatch (§5.1–§6.2).
