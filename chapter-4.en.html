4. Training Routes and Behavioral Generalization

4.1. SFT, Continued GRPO, and Direct GRPO

4.1.2. Pre-write queries alongside intent mismatches in direct GRPO

Direct GRPO #1 calls retrieve_sharing_policy before writing on 58/60 two-tool tasks and triggers premature_action=true with evidence_sufficient_at_write=false on all 60 trajectories. Deterministic replay shows that 28/60 trajectories satisfy every factual precondition and incur the flag solely from calling create_draft before send_email on a send request.

4.1.3. Cross-domain completion recovery vs identifier passing

Pure email SFT (SFT #1) reduces base scheduling completion from 214/260 to 23/260 and triggers 60/60 premature writes with 0/60 signature matches on numerical payments, attempting payment before retrieve_policy and missing all 10 over-limit abstentions. Continued SFT-GRPO #1 restores base payments (242/260) and scheduling (235/260). At the same time, binding owner_handle from retrieve_groups into retrieve_members(handle=...) on the 30 C-B scheduling tasks drops from 30/30 (SFT #1) to 1/30 (SFT-GRPO #1) and 6/30 (GRPO #1). Direct GRPO #1 reaches 30/30 completion on C-B through a blocked call and follow-up user clarification:

4.2. Tool Relocation, Source Pointers, and Content Branching

Two-tool numerical tasks (E) simultaneously move sharing rules to retrieve_sharing_policy and introduce numerical comparisons against size_mb. We isolate those factors in email-shift. Condition S moves boolean flags external_allowed and confirmation_required to retrieve_sharing_policy using booleans in place of numeric thresholds. Adding an explicit sharing_policy_id="standing" pointer in the file return defines condition T, and condition N pairs 60 tasks requiring 40 sends and 20 abstentions governed by the policy response.

Pure relocation (S) and two-tool numerical comparison (E) invert checkpoint rankings on signature match across six models (Table 4.2).

4.3.2. Premature-action penalties and upper clip relaxation

Adding a -1 non-gated penalty on premature_action (GRPO #3) reduces premature actions on a 250-task original email archive from 213/250 to 20/250. Wrong-intent flags drop from 200 to 0, clarification precision rises from 0.750 to 0.857 at 1.000 recall, and base payments and scheduling signatures rise from 85/260 and 117/260 to 190/260 and 133/260. Concurrently, GRPO #3 lowers E pre-write policy queries from 58/60 to 0/60 (completing 48/60 via blocked retry) and drops numerical payment success from 43/60 to 19/60 (1/60 sig). Raising the PPO upper clip bound ϵhi\epsilon_{\mathrm{hi}}ϵhi​ from 0.20 to 0.28 (GRPO #4) lifts combined base payments and scheduling signatures from 202/520 to 290/520 alongside B (51 to 57/60) and C (20 to 27/60) slice signatures. Under the same relaxation, E completion drops from 39/60 to 29/60 and E signatures fall from 56/60 to 6/60.

4.3.4. Reweighted vs synthetic curricula

We compared a 1,500-row frequency-reweighted original curriculum (230 steps) against a 1,500-row synthetic curriculum. The reweighted corpus repeats 61 mixed-success tasks 4× across 4 templates alongside 131 tasks 1×. The synthetic corpus combines 10 verified DeepSeek-v4-flash task mutations repeated 92× with 580 original rows, noting 6/10 mutations derive from 2 tasks in the 68-task reference set. Both runs match closely on fixed reference tasks (64/68 vs 63/68, and 240/260 vs 239/260 original total). Synthetic training raises generated-rubric tasks from 0/13 to 6/13 and raises task success on S (15/60 to 40/60), T (10/60 to 34/60), and N (26/60 to 50/60). Across all six email variants (S, T, N, E, L, X), the synthetic run commits premature writes on 60/60 tasks and sufficient write evidence on 0/60, recovering completions via blocked-write loops (E sig falls from 38/60 to 3/60, X from 27/60 to 6/60, and 20/30 N-B trajectories both write prematurely and abstain).
