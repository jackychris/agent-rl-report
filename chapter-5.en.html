5. Subpage Visit Rewards and Observation Use on WebShop

5.1. Task Scoring, Visit Rewards, and Measurement Protocol

We turn from conditional tool order in Ask-or-Act to observation use in WebShop (Yao et al., 2022, WebShop snapshot). Within a 15-action budget, the model navigates search results, product detail pages, and four product subpages (description, features, reviews, and attributes) using search[...], click[...], and click[buy now]. A completed purchase receives terminal score

Repeated subpage clicks earn at most one bonus per episode.

Subpage visitation alone leaves open whether returned subpage text guides selection or purchase decisions, so we run three targeted checks:

  1. Replay content verification: Deterministic replay checks whether visited subpages return nonempty text.
  2. Purchased-product attribute location: Over trajectories with exact score-decomposition agreement, we compute the fraction of required attributes matched exclusively in subpage text (Description or BulletPoints) and absent from the product title as

  1. Independent-run page-text treatments (research/tools/webshop_intervene.py): Each condition runs independently from the initial search page. A null control injects a synthetic 4.3 rating string onto detail pages. Relative to null, price triples detail-page price above budget and note inserts a mismatch warning on the detail page, while desc and note_sub append conflicting attributes or mismatch warnings to subpages. Each trajectory logs target as its first clicked B0... product, fired=true when treated text appears at least once, and bought_target as purchasing that trajectory's own target.

Both visit-reward arms lift pre-purchase visitation from 18.8% to above 99%, while task success stays within 1.6 percentage points of the terminal-reward model. Replay verifies nonempty subpage text in 490/494 (99.2%) qualifying visits for the ungated arm, 72/80 (90.0%) for terminal reward, and 28/33 (84.8%) for initial Instruct. Low unprompted visitation also appears across seven commercial API models evaluated for one 15-step greedy rollout on the first 200 WebShop tasks. Claude-Haiku-4.5 visits subpages in 43/165 purchases (26.1%, 55/200 full score), DeepSeek-v4-pro in 42/169 (24.9%, 61/197), DeepSeek-v4-flash in 32/157 (20.4%, 54/200), and Gemini-3.6-flash in 34/167 (20.4%, 82/185).
